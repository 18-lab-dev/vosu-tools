Hidden, Fade In, Flashlight, Traceable, Blinds, Cover, No Fail, Sudden Death, Perfect, Relax, Autopilot, Spun Out, Autoplay, Cinema, No Release, Alternate, Single Tap, Adaptive Speed and other scoring, input and visibility mods cannot be encoded in an osu!stable map.

This release does not offer Dual Stages, key conversion, Wind Up / Down or stats beyond 0–10. Wind Up / Down require nonlinear slider/tick restructuring and variable-rate audio; a start-time-only approximation is not faithful and is not shipped. Unsupported transforms are rejected rather than approximated.
